My process

From your first message to a finished project, in six steps.

  1. 1Tell me

    Send me a message: what you need, who it is for and when you need it. Links to websites or apps you like help a lot.

  2. 2Talk

    I read everything and reply with my questions. Together we agree on what to make and what can wait.

  3. 3Plan

    I write down every page or screen, what it has to say and what comes first. You check it before I start.

  4. 4Design

    I choose the colours, fonts and layout, and show you the look. We change it until it feels right.

  5. 5Build

    I build it with code and AI tools, review every part, and show you how it is going along the way.

  6. 6Check and launch

    I test it on a computer and a phone, fix what looks or works wrong, and put it online when you say it is ready.

Sage

Website · AI

Write what you like doing and get careers that fit, why each one fits, and a first step to take.

See it live (opens in a new tab) GitHub (opens in a new tab)

The challenge

It is hard to choose a career from a list of job titles. Sage starts from what you already enjoy instead.

How it works

You write a few words about your hobbies, and Google's Gemini AI suggests careers and explains each match using your own words.

Every career ends with one thing you can do next. The full report adds what a typical day is like, how people get into the job, and a plan for the first four weeks.

What it does

  • Free preview with three careers, no sign-up and no card
  • Accounts that save every search
  • Monthly and yearly plans with a 7-day trial, through Stripe
  • English, Spanish, French, German and Russian

The result

A live web app that anyone can try in the browser, in five languages, with a free preview and no sign-up.

Bunker 7

Game · Team project

A survival game you play in the browser. You have seven days to build a radio and get rescued.

Play it (opens in a new tab) GitHub (opens in a new tab)

The challenge

Make a game for a game jam, as a team of four, around one theme: "Bounce Back".

How it works

You are the last survivor in Bunker 7. Each day you climb out to collect wood, scrap and circuits among the zombies, and the radiation grows the farther you go from the hatch.

The theme became a rule: when you die you bounce back and wake up in the bunker, but only a few times.

What it does

  • One goal: finish the radio beacon and be alive on day 7
  • Thirst, hunger and radiation to manage on every trip
  • Bunker rooms you clear and upgrade one by one
  • Two more places to explore: the Old Mine and the Bomb Crater
  • Three difficulty levels

The result

A finished survival game that runs in the browser, published on itch.io.
